In the last century, a tourist from the States visited the famous Polish rabbi Hafez Hayyim. He was astonished to see that the rabbi's home was only a simple room filled with books. 

The only furniture was a table and a bench. 

"Rabbi, where is your furniture?" asked the tourist. 

"Where is yours?" replied Hafez. 

"Mine? But I'm only a visitor here." 

"So am I," said the rabbi.
