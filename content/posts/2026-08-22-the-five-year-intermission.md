This post is the missing announcement, written from the other side.

## The world moved too

The gap happens to bracket the strangest shift of my professional life.
When the last post went out in 2021, my toolkit was the classic
DevOps/cloud stack, and "AI" mostly meant a category of conference talks.
Writing this in 2026, a large language model helped resurrect the very
site you are reading — the dormant Hugo build, the 550-post WordPress-era
archive, the theme, the publishing pipeline. That story deserves its own
post; the point here is that the person writing in 2026 works in a
noticeably different world than the one who stopped in 2021.

## Where I am now

The living version of "where am I now" has its own page: [/now](/now/).
That page gets updated; this post stays frozen as the marker between the
two eras of this blog.

## What this blog becomes

Two things change with the restart:

1. **Short notes are now first-class.** There is a new [Notes](/notes/)
   stream for things that are worth writing down but not worth an essay —
   TILs, links, half-thoughts. The long-form posts continue when there is
   something long-form to say.
2. **The archive stays.** All 555 posts back to 2006 remain exactly where
   they are, WordPress-era formatting warts and all. They are the record.

The gap years may get backfilled with a few posts under the
[catching-up](/tags/catching-up/) tag — what changed in the tools, in the
work, in the reading — written when one of them wants to be written, not
on a schedule.

Mostly this is for me. But if someone else is reading: welcome back, and
so am I.
